# 8-Week MVP Development Plan

1. Week 1 — Foundation and product facts: Python package, CI, database, SKU CRUD, sourced facts and verification.
2. Week 2 — Asset library: uploads, object storage, metadata extraction, SKU association and tags.
3. Week 3 — Product and creative workflow: fact extraction/validation, brief, English script and storyboard.
4. Week 4 — Asset matching: shot classification, search, matching and missing-shot/reshoot list.
5. Week 5 — Rendering: EDL, FFmpeg, subtitles/audio and enhancement adapter interface.
6. Week 6 — Quality and approvals: technical QC, claim/provenance checks, review and revision flow.
7. Week 7 — Publication and feedback: manual TikTok publication log, metrics entry and basic dashboard.
8. Week 8 — Real-world pilot: compare baseline and Agent-assisted workflows across a small set of real SKUs.

## Suggested responsibilities
Product/project lead: factory coordination, product facts and requirements. Backend/AI engineer: API, workflow, Agents and adapters. Video/full-stack engineer: asset processing, FFmpeg, UI and QC. Content operators: English localization, final review, publishing and metrics.

## Definition of Done
Code, tests, error handling, logs, documentation and a real-SKU acceptance check are complete.

## After MVP
Only after internal evidence supports value should the team prioritize multi-tenancy, customer onboarding, roles, billing, automated publishing and external integrations.
