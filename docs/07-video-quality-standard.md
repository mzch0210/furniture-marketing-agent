# Furniture Marketing Video Quality Standard

## Initial default profile
- 25–27 seconds (campaign-dependent)
- 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps
- MP4 / H.264
- English captions
- Hook → Body → CTA
- Around seven shot groups as a starting template

These are internal defaults, not universal platform requirements.

## Suggested shot sequence
1. Hook: immediate problem, result or compelling product view.
2. Product reveal: establish the actual product.
3. Feature demonstration: show a real, verified function.
4. Detail: material, construction or finish only when confirmed.
5. Use case: realistic usage context.
6. Proof: demonstrable feature or approved factory/product evidence.
7. CTA: clear next action.

## Product truth
Keep geometry, construction, color and finish faithful to source. Do not add accessories or functions that are not included. Never invent dimensions, materials, load limits, certifications or guarantees. If enhancement creates ambiguity, use original footage or require human review.

## Automated checks
File readability, duration, dimensions, FPS, codec, audio, subtitles, black-frame detection, SKU match, claim-source availability, unverified-claim detection, CTA and asset provenance.

## Human scorecard (internal rubric)
Product truth/visual fidelity 25; English naturalness 20; hook 15; visual quality 15; clarity 10; pacing 10; CTA 5. Suggested: 85+ publishable, 75–84 revise, below 75 rework. Calibrate this rubric during the pilot.
